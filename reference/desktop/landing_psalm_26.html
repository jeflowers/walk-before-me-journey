Emmaus Road • Psalm 26

Walk
Before Me

A journey through Psalm 26 in seven waypoints

Seven waypoints trace the path of integrity from the covenant ground of Genesis 17 to the even place of Psalm 26:12. Each waypoint sets the text first, then asks one question we carry into the week.

Waypoint I

Covenant Ground

"I am the Almightie God: walke before me, and be thou perfect."

Genesis 17:1

Does the walk begin with our strength, or His name?

Waypoint II

The Prayer Beneath

"Let integrity and uprightnesse preserve me: for I wait on thee."

Psalm 25:21

Is integrity a claim of sinlessness, or a plea for preservation?

Waypoint III

The Walk and The Trust

"Judge me, O LORD, for I have walked in mine integrity: I have trusted also in the LORD: therfore I shall not slide."

Psalm 26:1, 3

Which holds the man: the walk, or the trust?

Waypoint IV

The Door Opened

"Examine me, O LORD, and prove me; try my reins and my heart."

Psalm 26:2

Do we open the inner rooms to the One we trust?

Note: Tsaraph refined by fire
Waypoint V

The Habitation He Loves

"LORD, I have loved the habitation of thy house, and the place where thine honour dwelleth."

Psalm 26:4-8

Does our separation serve our loves?

Waypoint VI

The Redemption

"But as for me, I will walke in mine integrity: redeeme me, and be mercifull unto me."

Psalm 26:9-11

Where does our integrity meet His mercy?

Fulfilled in Christ
Waypoint VII

The Even Place

"My foot standeth in an even place: in the congregations will I blesse the LORD."

Psalm 26:12

When does private integrity become public praise?

Not by might, nor by power, but by my spirit, saith the LORD of hosts.

Zechariah 4:6
