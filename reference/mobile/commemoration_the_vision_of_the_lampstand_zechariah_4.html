The vision of the golden lampstand serves as a profound architectural blueprint of divine sustenance. Through the meticulous examination of the olive trees feeding directly into the bowl, we uncover a continuous, unending supply of the Spirit. This journey has dismantled the reliance on human infrastructure ("might and power") and reoriented the builder towards the singular source of true efficacy. The mountain becomes a plain not through exertion, but through the vitalizing flow of the anointed ones standing by the Lord of the whole earth.
