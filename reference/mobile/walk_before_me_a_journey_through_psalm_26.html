The Walk and The Trust

"Judge me, O LORD, for I have walked in mine integrity: I have trusted also in the LORD: therfore I shall not slide."

Psalm 26:1, 3

Which holds the man: the walk, or the trust?

Psalm 26:2

Do we open the inner rooms to the One we trust?
